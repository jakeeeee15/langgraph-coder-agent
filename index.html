How Nova operates

A transparent loop that keeps you in control while the agent does the heavy lifting.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Nova connects to your workspace, reads permissions, and maps available tools and data sources.

  2. 2

    Plan

    It decomposes your objective into tasks, selects the right integrations, and creates a reviewable plan.

  3. 3

    Execute

    The agent runs the workflow step by step, keeping logs, artifacts, and context current.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Outputs are checked against constraints, quality rules, and source references before delivery.

  5. 5

    Learn

    Approved outcomes improve future runs through memory, templates, and feedback loops.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Nova autonomous or just a chatbot?

Nova is an agent. It can plan multi-step work, call tools, verify outputs, and escalate to humans when policy or uncertainty requires approval.

How does Nova keep actions safe?

Every run uses scoped permissions, verification checks, audit logs, and configurable approval gates before sensitive actions execute.

Can Nova connect to our existing tools?

Yes. Nova integrates with common productivity, communication, CRM, support, and knowledge tools through native connectors and APIs.

Does it store our data?

You control data retention and memory scope. Enterprise plans offer private deployment options and stronger governance controls.

What happens if Nova makes a mistake?

Nova logs every step, flags low-confidence outputs, and can request human review. Approved corrections improve future runs.
