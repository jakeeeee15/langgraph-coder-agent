Frequently asked questions

Learn how Jake works, what it can do, and how teams use it safely.

What is Jake?

Jake is an AI agent designed to help with everyday work tasks such as drafting, summarizing, analyzing, researching, and automating workflows.

Can Jake connect to my tools?

Yes. Jake can be configured to work with common tools like email, documents, chat platforms, spreadsheets, CRM systems, and automation workflows.

Is Jake suitable for teams?

Jake supports shared workspaces, role-based access, approval flows, and context management so teams can collaborate safely.

How does Jake handle privacy?

Jake can be configured with permission controls, audit logging, data retention settings, and human review steps to support privacy and governance.

Can I try Jake before committing?

Yes. Use the interactive demo on this page or contact the team to request a guided pilot for your workflow.
